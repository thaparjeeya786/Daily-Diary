## What is Sentiment Analysis?
Sentiment Analysis is the process of determining the emotional polarity of text (positive, negative, neutral).  
It can also capture deeper emotions such as **happiness, anger, or sadness**.  

There are three main approaches:  
- **Rule-based** – Uses predefined rules and lexicons.  
- **Automatic** – Uses ML algorithms for classification.  
- **Hybrid** – Combines both approaches for better accuracy.  

## Steps Involved
1. **Text Preprocessing** – Clean the text (remove punctuation, stopwords, etc.).  
2. **Tokenization** – Split text into individual words or tokens.  
3. **Feature Extraction** – Represent text using Bag of Words or TF-IDF.  
4. **Model Training** – Use classifiers like Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes, or LSTMs.  
5. **Prediction** – Classify unseen text as positive, negative, or neutral.

- **Social Media Monitoring** – Quickly track reactions to events or campaigns.
