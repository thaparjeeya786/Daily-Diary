## History & Evolution of AI
- 1956: The term "Artificial Intelligence" was introduced by John McCarthy.  
- 1980s: Machine learning techniques enhanced AI's capability.  
- 2000s–Present: With the explosion of data and better computing power, AI has advanced rapidly and is now widely used in healthcare, finance, robotics, and daily applications.

## Types of Artificial Intelligence (Based on Capabilities)

### 1. Narrow AI (Weak AI)
Designed for a specific task, such as voice commands or recommendations.  
These systems perform well within their set boundaries but cannot generalize beyond them.
Examples:
- Voice assistants like Siri or Alexa  
- Facial recognition in security systems  
- Recommendation engines on Netflix, Amazon, etc.

### 2.General AI (Strong AI)
Represents systems with human-like intelligence.  
They can understand, learn, and adapt to new tasks, similar to human behavior.

Potential Applications:
- Robots that learn and adapt in real-time  
- AI systems that diagnose and solve medical problems across domains

### 3. Superintelligence (Super AI)
A hypothetical AI that surpasses human intelligence in every aspect—problem-solving, creativity, and emotion.  
Such AI could have its own desires, emotions, and beliefs, and operate autonomously.
