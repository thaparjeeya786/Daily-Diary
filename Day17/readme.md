## What is NLP?
NLP (Natural Language Processing) combines linguistics, computer science, and AI to allow computers to work with human language. It makes interaction between humans and machines more natural and effective.  

## Importance of NLP
- Bridges the gap between human communication and computer systems.  
- Drives applications like chatbots, translation tools, and voice assistants (Siri, Alexa).  
- Used in sentiment analysis, spam filters, and automated recommendations.  

## NLP Techniques
- **Tokenization** – Splitting text into smaller units like words or sentences.  
- **Stemming & Lemmatization** – Reducing words to their root or base form.  
- **Stopword Removal** – Filtering out common words like *is*, *the*, *and*.  
- **Bag of Words / TF-IDF** – Converting text into numerical form.  
- **Named Entity Recognition (NER)** – Identifying names, locations, dates, etc.
